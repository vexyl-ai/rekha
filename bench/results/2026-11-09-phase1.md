# Phase 1 baseline — 2026-11-09

Rekha `05bdf3e` (bilevel spline mode, defaults) against Potrace 1.16 and VTracer 1.0.0-alpha.4 on the synthetic corpus, scored by `rekha-bench` (rsvg-convert rasterisation, PSNR against the input PNG). Produced with `make bench`.

## Phase 1 goals

Goal: on the five bilevel shapes, Rekha within 2 dB of Potrace and at most 1.5× its node count. **All five pass**; Rekha beats Potrace on PSNR on every goal image with fewer nodes.

| Image | Potrace PSNR | Rekha PSNR | ΔdB (goal ≥ −2) | Potrace Nodes | Rekha Nodes | ratio (goal ≤ 1.5) | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| circle-aa | 30.76 | 33.37 | +2.61 | 13 | 6 | 0.46 | pass |
| circle-hard | 28.54 | 29.26 | +0.72 | 11 | 6 | 0.55 | pass |
| rounded-rect | 30.49 | 35.96 | +5.47 | 10 | 10 | 1.00 | pass |
| star-5 | 35.75 | 38.34 | +2.59 | 22 | 11 | 0.50 | pass |
| thin-lines | 25.66 | 28.17 | +2.51 | 30 | 17 | 0.57 | pass |

Known gaps (not goals, follow-ups):

- `checkerboard-2px`: Rekha emits one path per 2×2 square (8192 paths); Potrace merges diagonally touching squares with its turn policy. Both are faithful; a per-colour path merge is a Phase 2 item.
- Colour images (`gradient-h`, `three-layer-stack`, `two-colour-abut`) are thresholded to bilevel in Phase 1, as Potrace does; VTracer's colour results are the Phase 2 reference.
