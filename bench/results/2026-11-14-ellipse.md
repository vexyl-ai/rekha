The 21 real images are byte-identical: no free loop on them is an ellipse that the circle snap declined (the Pokéball's rings stay circles, as the 1 % axis-ratio floor intends), so nothing changed and nothing regressed. The two synthetics are the gain: `ellipse-aa` from 40.38 to 45.43 dB and `ellipse-rot` from 43.37 to 43.87, each at four nodes before and after; the free refinement already found four cubics there but left them 0.28 to 0.34 px off the true ellipse, and the snap puts every point within 0.2 px. The logo node and arc-share table is unchanged from `2026-11-14-primitives-v2.md`.

What the bench says about the feature: it is correct and cheap (one 5×5 fit per free loop the circle declines) but idle on this corpus, whose logos are circles, arcs and free curves. It earns its place on icons and illustrations with ellipses, which this set lacks; adding such an image to the bench set is the next step if the pass is to be measured on real material.
