# Flat preset, 2026-11-14

Twenty-one images as in `2026-11-14-rescue.md`, PSNR over white by rsvg-convert, with the path count and the number of distinct fills. Both columns are `--refine` on branch `flat-preset`: the poster at `--color-tolerance 0.06`, and `--preset flat` (the photo preset's 0.04 segmentation, then `region.Flatten`: adjacent shades of one hue within 0.12 of lightness and 23° of hue merged into the larger shade's colour, neutrals and chromatics never merged).

A flat look loses PSNR by design: the shading the quantiser kept at 0.04 is what the merge removes, and the metric scores the shading. The table is here to show what it costs, and where; the preset is for the posterised look, not for fidelity.

Mean −2.50 dB, 3 wins (blue-purple-m +0.76 at 160 → 48 paths, small-glyphs +0.85, blue-u +0.17), 16 losses, 2 unchanged; paths 2413 → 1303 over the set (54 %), fills 241 → 190. The largest losses are the images that are shading: gradient-shapes −10.4 (ramps become two or three bands), the Pokéball −9.2 (its two reds become one red, its grey shadow joins the white: five fills to three, the flat-icon drawing of it), the v-dots logo −6.0, unicorn −4.1. Where a logo is already flat (thin-lines, teal-blue, blue-u, the q ribbon) the two agree within a quarter of a decibel with fewer paths.

The boy render (1200×2198, the cleaned copy) under `--preset flat --refine`: 28.58 dB, 331 paths, 20 fills, the iris and the strap brown, the badge blue; the poster at 0.06 refined scores 32.37 dB with 408 paths and 26 fills, keeping the shading. Raising the poster tolerance instead merges across hue before across lightness: at 0.10 the skin is grey.

The first cut gave a merged group the mean of its pixels; the group now takes its largest member's own colour. The Pokéball scored the same either way (30.39 against 30.37), since the merge is the cost, but its red is now its own red rather than a value between its two reds.
