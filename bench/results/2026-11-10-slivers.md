# Anti-aliasing slivers judged by their pixels (2026-11-10)

A thin component between two colours used to be absorbed only when its
*palette* colour lay within the colour tolerance of the OKLab segment
between its neighbours. Anti-aliased pixels between two colours quantise
to whatever palette entry is nearest, and on a poster that can be a third
colour of the image: on the bird JPEG the pixels between cyan #26b8db and
navy #023a82, such as (10, 114, 178), are 0.002–0.014 from the sRGB
cyan–navy mixture yet nearest to the bird's teal #349184, and teal is
0.093 from the segment (tolerance 0.08), so the slivers stayed as dashed
teal strips along every feather edge. `region.Cleanup` now also absorbs a
thin component whose *mean pixel colour* is within tolerance of the sRGB
mixture of its two longest-border neighbours (`colour.MixDist`).

Bird (4001², poster): 126 → 62 paths, PSNR 30.38 → 30.34 dB; at 2000²
in the browser 214 → 37 paths and the feather edges are clean at 4×.
