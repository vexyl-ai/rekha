## Cutout and the stroke stopgap

Run by hand (rsvg-convert at 1:1, PSNR against the PNG over white; `--hierarchical cutout` with `--cutout-stroke W`, and the stacked default for comparison):

```
bin/rekha --preset poster [--refine] --hierarchical cutout --cutout-stroke W -o out.svg testdata/corpus/synthetic/<img>.png
bin/rekha --preset poster [--refine] -o out.svg testdata/corpus/synthetic/<img>.png     # stacked
rsvg-convert -w 256 -h 256 out.svg -o out.png
```

The goal "cutout with the stroke within 1 dB of stacked" is not met at any width. Two independently fitted curves of one boundary leave a sub-pixel seam that a 1:1 rasterisation sees on every edge, and refinement, which brings each curve onto the anti-aliasing, narrows but cannot close it (seams of ~0.1 px remain). The 0.5 px stroke hides seams when the SVG is viewed enlarged but at 1:1 thickens every edge by a quarter pixel, which costs 2–7 dB; 0.1–0.2 px is neutral. After review the default is 0 (opt-in); shared boundaries between abutting cutout shapes remain the real fix. The table was measured before the review fix pass changed the handle priors and merge criterion; the goal table below it was re-run after.

## Notes

- The review fix pass that followed the first run: the short-handle barrier no longer rewards handles beyond a third of the chord and a handle-order prior keeps handle projections inside the chord and ordered (knotted caps on a 1-px line, crossed handles in `poster-discs`); a merge must hold over its own window as well as globally (`rounded-rect` 57.5 → 50.9 dB, `poster-discs` 36.9 → 37.8, `thin-lines` 32.4 → 33.5); the cutout stroke default is 0. Refined goldens are compared as geometry because the plain fit already differs by ulps between amd64 and arm64.
- Three defects found by the first run and fixed before it was recorded: (1) every 2-px square of `checkerboard-2px` was replaced by a circle (an absolute 0.5 px circle tolerance accepts a square of radius 1.2; the tolerance is now also capped at a tenth of the radius), which had dropped the image from exact to 20 dB; (2) hole masks zeroed fully-covered edge pixels whose second colour was not the layer's, costing cutout refinement 7 dB on `logo-holes` (in-test 39.8 → 33.0; now 55.1); (3) the self-intersection prior and the merge pass were linear in the outline length per energy evaluation, so a 3.2-megapixel logo took 140 s (now 14 s; plain fit 1.4 s).
- `rekha-photo` is unchanged from the Phase 2 baseline and omitted above; the full table is below.
- Refinement time is 50–290 ms per 256×256 image including segmentation; `checkerboard-2px` (8192 shapes, 0.8 s) is the slowest and is left exact.
- `thin-lines` under bw: 28.17 → 33.47 dB (length prior normalised by the initial length; the raw prior shrank thin shapes).
- `gradient-h` under poster: +0.08 dB — bands are rectangles with hard edges; refinement has nothing to recover and the merge/split pass adds four nodes.
