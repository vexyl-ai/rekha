# Phase 2 baseline — 2026-11-09

Rekha `8c0c48c` (after the review fix pass) against Potrace 1.16 and VTracer 1.0.0-alpha.4 on the synthetic corpus, scored by `rekha-bench` (rsvg-convert rasterisation, PSNR against the input PNG). Produced with `make bench`. Three Rekha rows per image: `rekha` (bw preset), `rekha-poster`, `rekha-photo`, all in spline mode with preset defaults.

Preset values used: poster = stacked, MaxColors 0 (tolerance-bounded), ColorTolerance 0.08, ColorPrecision 6, FilterSpeckle 4, FitTolerance 1.0; photo = stacked, ColorTolerance 0.04, FilterSpeckle 16, FitTolerance 1.5; both CornerThreshold 60. Stacked siblings extend under later siblings by ceil(2√2·FitTolerance)+1 px.

## Phase 2 goals

Goal: on the five colour synthetics, the poster preset within 2 dB PSNR of VTracer and at most 1.5× its node count; on `gradient-h`, the photo preset within 2 dB of VTracer at no more than 2× its paths. **All six pass.** Rekha beats VTracer on PSNR on every goal image except `aa-abut` (−0.49 dB, within the goal), with fewer nodes everywhere.

Notes:

- Changes since the first baseline (`ef37f57`): the Wu tolerance stop keeps small subjects and ignores anti-aliasing mixtures and noise tails; cells are labelled by the sRGB midpoint between their two nearest entries; saddles resolve to the minority colour in tracing and cleanup alike. The colour presets now match the bw preset on the bilevel synthetics: `rounded-rect` and `thin-lines` are identical, `circle-aa` and `star-5` within 0.4 dB (before: 4–7 dB below bw).
- `aa-abut` before the sibling-extension fix scored 36.43 dB: the two triangles each had an anti-aliased edge and white bled through the join. Extending the first colour under the second closed the gap.
- `checkerboard-2px` under poster is pixel-identical: one black path (the squares connect at corners) and one white path for the squares inside it. The bw preset still emits 8192 paths. Under photo FilterSpeckle 16 erases the 4-pixel squares.
- `noisy-disc` under poster labels better than bw (pixel mode 28.12 dB against bw 27.56) but its spline fit lands at 25.48 dB: the fitter settles on a worse local solution on the jagged boundary than it did for the previous labelling (28.28). Fit quality on noisy outlines is the Phase 3 refinement stage's job. Under photo (ColorTolerance 0.04) the noise resolves into 244 regions at 22.80 dB; tolerance values are starting points.
- Colour presets take about 110 ms per 256×256 image against 6 ms for bw: the lookup table over all 6-bit cells and k-means over occupied cells dominate and are size-independent. Follow-up.
