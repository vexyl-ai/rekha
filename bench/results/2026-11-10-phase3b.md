"rsvg 4× box" renders the SVG at 1024×1024 and box-filters to 256; "in-repo 4×" is the test rasteriser (`TestCutoutWithin3dBOfStacked`: point-sampled at 4×, every sub-sample belongs to exactly one shape).

| Goal | Measured | Result |
|---|---|---|
| cutout within 3 dB of stacked (in-repo rasteriser) | worst gap 0.5 dB | pass |
| cutout within 3 dB of stacked (`make bench`: rsvg 1:1) | gaps 13.1 / 10.9 / 15.5 / 3.7 dB, unchanged from Phase 3 | **fail** |
| each image ≥ 3 dB above its Phase 3 cutout value (rsvg 1:1) | −0.1 / +1.0 / −0.1 / +2.1 dB | **fail** |

The geometry is right and the rendering is the limit: a renderer that anti-aliases each shape on its own composites a boundary pixel as α of the top colour over (1−α) of the bottom colour over the page, so the page bleeds through with weight (1−α)·α along every shared edge, whatever the curves are. Supersampling shrinks the bleed in proportion (the 4× box column) and a point-sampled renderer has none. The Phase 3 stroke stopgap still trades this bleed against thickened edges: with identical boundaries 0.1–0.2 px is neutral to +1 dB at 1:1, 0.5 px costs 0–8 dB, 1 px costs 5–13 dB. Stacked output remains the default and the recommendation for viewing at 1:1; cutout is for editing.

## Notes

- rsvg-convert at 1:1 is not a reliable judge above about 55 dB: the snapped rounded rectangle measures 0.4 dB from its geometric ideal under the in-repo 16×/32× rasteriser and 2.9 dB above the refined curve, yet rsvg ranks them the other way. Numbers above 55 dB in the results table say "very close", not which of two outputs is closer.
- The refinement optimiser is chaotic on `logo-holes`: a finite-difference step change of 1e-5 moves the result by 0.8 dB, and arm64 and amd64 reach different states (rounded-rect primitives off: 54.7 vs 52.0 dB in-repo). Refine goldens are therefore compared as geometry with a PSNR floor, not byte for byte.
- `big-poster` under bw thresholds six colours to a single blotchy layer (10.4 dB for every bilevel tool); the row is there for the timing and the node count, not the quality.
- `vectormagic` and `vectorizer-ai` rows read `bench/reference/<tool>/<name>.svg` or `.png`; this run had none. The procedure for recording them is in `docs/benchmark.md`.
