# Slack for small gradient regions (2026-11-19)

`slackTol` in `internal/gradient`: a region under an eighth of the image
may miss its gradient model by up to twice `--gradient-tolerance`,
growing as `tol·√(np / (8·n))`. Spec §2h.

Image: a white wolf logo shaded in soft greys (user's
`tests/0a4a94778960cea1092bbdd364915633.jpg`, 1024², JPEG). Scored
against the source with `rsvg-convert` at 1:1, PSNR over white.

## The problem

| trace | PSNR dB | background dB | chin/neck dB | look |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| photo+gradients, tol 0.03 (auto's winner) | 29.34 | 39.55 | 26.11 | chin and neck shading in flat steps |
| same, gradient tolerance 0.04 | 28.43 | 33.08 | 26.17 | shading back, vignette off |
| same, gradient tolerance 0.05 | 28.29 | 32.74 | 26.17 | shading back, vignette off |

Knobs that did nothing (under 0.1 dB on the wolf, nothing elsewhere):
boundary gate on soft share instead of mean step; lone bands sampled by
the multi-stop model; fitting against a 3×3 or 5×5 box blur; stop
simplification held at 0.03 under a looser tolerance.

## The fix

photo + gradients, speckle 2, no refine:

| image | before dB | after dB | gradients | bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| wolf | 29.34 | 29.10 | 41+5 → 44+2 | 235 407 → 221 258 |
| 8_1sasa11 | 41.57 | 41.57 | identical | identical |
| eight other logos in tests/ | — | — | byte-identical | byte-identical |

`--auto` (photo+gradients+refine wins both times): 31.51 → 31.29 dB,
517 → 486 kB. The chin's shadow and the shoulder strand are one ramp
each; the light band along the neck stays flat (a curved strand shaded
along its length fits no straight or radial field).

Every golden byte-identical; `go test ./...` green on arm64 and amd64.
