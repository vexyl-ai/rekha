# A ramp's plateau (2026-11-11)

A shading that reaches its end colour and holds it quantises into a
ramp band and a flat band with no step across their boundary. The bend
pass refused the pair, because a flat side wide enough to tell votes
against a join (a flat colour next to a ramp across a soft step is a
shape of its own), so the letter G's ring met its dark end at a hard
seam, visible as a cut in the shading.

Edges now count their continuous pixel pairs (a step of two counts or
less), and across a boundary that is continuous along all but a tenth
of its length a flat side no longer refuses: it is the ramp's end, and
the sampled stops hold its level. A flat behind a blurred edge is
refused as before.

Along rows through the G's ring the output now follows the source
within one count into the plateau.

Not fixed: the Firefox body's remaining ragged seams. Those boundaries
are crisp along most of their length and continuous along the rest
(7–29 % of their pairs within two counts): overlapping translucent
shapes in the original, one edge hard and the other a fade. A gate over
the whole boundary cannot admit them, and joining them would put the
hard part inside a smooth model; smoothing the label boundary through
the soft part is the next step.
