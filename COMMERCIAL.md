## When you need the commercial licence

You need it if you distribute, sell or host something built on Rekha
and do not want to release that something's source under the AGPL-3.0.
Typical cases: a desktop or mobile app that embeds the library or the
WebAssembly build; a web service or API that traces images for its
users; a SaaS product with a "convert to SVG" feature; an internal tool
at a company that will not open its code.

You do not need it to use the command-line tool or the demo page on
your own images, to evaluate Rekha, or to contribute to it.

## Why dual licensing

The paid licences fund development of the open version. Every
improvement paid for by a commercial customer lands in the public
repository under the AGPL-3.0; there is no private fork of the tracer.
